01 / What it does

Less budgeting. More knowing.

Most finance apps hand you a pie chart and wish you luck. Lumen does the reading for you and only speaks up when something is worth your attention.

  • Insights in plain English

    A short note when something changes, written the way a sharp friend would say it. No jargon, no dashboards to decode.

    "Takeaway is up 23% this month, mostly on Thursdays."

  • Autopilot savings

    Lumen studies your cash flow and moves small, safe amounts into savings on the days you will not miss them.

    Moved $35 to "Lisbon trip" · balance stays above $900

  • Subscription radar

    Every recurring charge in one list, with price hikes and free trials about to convert flagged before they bill you.

    Trial ends in 2 days: StreamBox, $14.99/mo

  • 30-day forecast

    See your balance a month ahead, with bills, paydays and habits already counted, so nothing lands as a surprise.

    Lowest point: $612 on the 28th, after rent

  • Shared spaces

    Run a household without merging your lives. Share the accounts you choose and keep the rest entirely your own.

    You and Dev are $48 under on groceries

  • Private by design

    Connections are read-only and encrypted end to end. We make money from subscriptions, never from your data.

    0 advertisers · 0 data brokers · since day one

      Fair things to ask before you connect a bank.

      Still unsure? Write to hello@lumen.example and a person will reply within a day.

      No. Lumen is a layer on top of the accounts you already have. Your money stays exactly where it is today, and autopilot transfers only ever move between accounts that are in your own name.

      Through your bank's own secure sign-in. You log in on your bank's page, and the bank gives us a read-only token. We never see or store your password, and you can revoke access from your bank or from Lumen at any time.

      Three things: it categorises transactions, spots changes worth mentioning, and forecasts your balance. Your transactions are not used to train models shared with anyone else, and they are never sold or shown to advertisers.

      Only inside rules you set. You choose a maximum per transfer and a balance it must never dip below. The first three transfers need your approval, and you can pause autopilot with one tap.
